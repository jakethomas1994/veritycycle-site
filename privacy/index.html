Verity Cycle

Privacy policy

Last updated 21 October 2026

In short: everything you log in Verity Cycle stays on your iPhone and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud account. We never see it, and we don't collect, sell or share any personal data.

Who we are

Verity Cycle is made by Jake Thomas, an independent developer in the United Kingdom ("we", "us"). You can contact us at support@veritycycle.app.

What you enter

You can log pain and where it is on your body, cycle events, medication, mood, sleep, food, how symptoms affect your day, notes, your first name and a health profile. This is health information, so we've designed the app so that it never reaches us.

  • It is stored on your iPhone.
  • If iCloud is turned on for Verity Cycle, it is also synced to your private iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit. Apple stores it under Apple's privacy policy, and we cannot access it.
  • If iCloud is off or unavailable, your data stays only on your iPhone. You can check this in Settings → iCloud sync.

Reports, exports and backups

You can create a PDF report for your GP, a CSV export and a full backup file. These are made on your iPhone. They only leave it if you choose to share them, for example by email or AirDrop. Once shared, they are handled by whoever you send them to.

Reminders

Reminders and weekly summaries are scheduled on your iPhone. They are not sent from our servers.

What we don't do

  • No accounts with us.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking tools.
  • No selling or sharing of data, because we don't hold any.
  • No connection to Apple Health.

Links and videos

The app links to charities, community spaces such as Reddit, and educational videos on YouTube. Video thumbnails are loaded from YouTube, which means YouTube can see your device's internet address when they load, as with any website. Opening a link takes you to that service, and its own privacy policy applies. None of your health logs are shared with these services.

Crash reports

If you turn on "Share with App Developers" in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements, Apple may share anonymous crash reports with us. These contain no health logs. You can turn this off at any time.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law (UK GDPR) you have rights over your personal data. Because we don't hold any of your data, you control it directly in the app:

  • See and export it: Settings → Export as CSV or Export full backup.
  • Delete it: Settings → Delete all data & reset. This removes it from your iPhone and from your iCloud copy.

For data Apple holds in your iCloud account, contact Apple. If you're unhappy with how your data is handled, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Children

Verity Cycle is not intended for children under 13.

Not medical advice

Verity Cycle helps you record your symptoms. It does not diagnose or treat any condition.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top. If a change matters to how your data is handled, we'll tell you in the app.
