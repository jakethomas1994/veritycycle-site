Common questions

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone and, if iCloud is turned on for Verity Cycle, in your own private iCloud account. We can't see it. You can check the sync status in Settings → iCloud sync.

How do I move my data to a new iPhone?

If iCloud sync is on, sign in to the same Apple Account on the new iPhone and install Verity Cycle. Otherwise, use Settings → Export full backup (JSON), save the file, then Settings → Import backup on the new phone.

How do I share my symptoms with my GP?

Go to Settings → Report export to create a PDF report you can print, email or show at your appointment.

How do I delete everything?

Settings → Delete all data & reset removes everything Verity Cycle has stored on your device and in iCloud.

Is Verity Cycle medical advice?

No. Verity Cycle helps you record and describe your symptoms. It does not diagnose or treat any condition. Always speak to a healthcare professional about your health. If you are in severe pain or think you need urgent help, call NHS 111 or 999.
